# Point 13 — Industries

Statut : validé par l’utilisateur. Passage au point 14 autorisé.

## Message

**Built for ambitious businesses.**

Different buyers. Different decisions. A growth strategy shaped around your market.

Les dix cartes du brief présentent chacune une promesse, les enjeux marketing et IA du secteur, puis trois axes de travail. Les textes décrivent une approche proposée, sans revendiquer de références clients ou de certifications non documentées.

## Textes des cartes

### SaaS

**Turn product research into qualified demos.**

Help buyers compare use cases, integrations and alternatives. Connect search content, AI visibility and lifecycle automation to the path from trial to adoption.

Product discovery · Demo conversion · Lifecycle automation.

### E-commerce

**Help shoppers find the right product.**

Structure product and category information for search and AI discovery. Align acquisition, product pages and retention workflows to improve the shopping journey.

Product data · Shopping intent · Retention.

### Professional Services

**Make your expertise easier to choose.**

Answer high-intent questions with clear service pages and expert-led content. Qualify enquiries and follow up while keeping personal relationships central.

Expert content · Qualified enquiries · CRM workflows.

### Finance

**Make complex offers easier to understand.**

Create clear, reviewable explanations for buyers comparing financial services. Keep sources, expert approval and traceability central to content and AI-assisted workflows.

Offer clarity · Editorial review · Attribution.

### Real Estate

**Connect local searches to relevant enquiries.**

Improve the discoverability of listings and local expertise. Organise property information and route enquiries to the right team with timely follow-up.

Local visibility · Listing content · Enquiry routing.

### Healthcare

**Help people find clear, reliable information.**

Make services and locations easier to discover. Build expert-reviewed content and streamline appointment enquiries with careful handling of personal information.

Service discovery · Expert review · Appointment journeys.

### Education

**Guide prospective learners from research to enrolment.**

Answer questions about programmes, outcomes and entry requirements. Align search content and follow-up with the time learners need to make a decision.

Programme content · Enrolment journeys · Lead nurturing.

### Local Businesses

**Turn nearby searches into visits and bookings.**

Keep service, location and opening-hour information consistent. Improve local pages and simplify the path from a search result to a call or booking.

Local search · Business information · Bookings.

### Agencies

**Increase delivery capacity without losing oversight.**

Connect reporting, research and repeatable production workflows. Use AI assistance with human review and keep each client’s data and approvals clearly separated.

Reporting · Delivery workflows · Human review.

### B2B

**Support every step of a longer buying decision.**

Address the questions of different stakeholders with useful content. Connect search visibility, lead qualification and CRM data to understand pipeline contribution.

Buying committees · Lead qualification · Pipeline measurement.

## Composition et parcours

Deux colonnes sur grand écran, une colonne sous 768 px. Chaque carte possède un numéro, un H3, une promesse courte, un paragraphe et trois axes de travail. Les textes restent visibles sans JavaScript et sans survol.

Les surfaces et bordures s’éclaircissent légèrement au survol en 220 ms. La préférence de réduction des animations désactive cette transition. Les cartes n’ont pas de hauteur fixe et ne simulent pas de liens vers des pages encore inexistantes.

Le CTA final **Book a Strategy Call** conduit au repère `#call` du prototype. Sa destination finale sera `/book-call`. Le lien « Industries » du header rejoint désormais la grille réelle de cet aperçu.

Les pages `/industries` et `/industries/[slug]` seront créées au point 19. La liste associe des secteurs, des types d’entreprise et le marché transversal B2B, conformément au brief ; les cartes peuvent donc se recouper.

## Aperçu

Ouvrir `13-industries.html` pour examiner le prototype cumulatif jusqu’au point 13. Les scripts des points 9, 10 et 11 restent utilisés par les sections précédentes ; la section Industries ne nécessite aucun script supplémentaire.
