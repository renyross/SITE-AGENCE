    How this scenario is calculated

    Additional traffic = monthly traffic × organic share × (1 − AI visibility score / 100) × 20%.

    The 20% factor is a fixed modelling assumption, not a benchmark. The relationship between AI visibility and traffic is hypothetical.

    Additional conversions = additional traffic × conversion rate. Revenue opportunity = unrounded additional conversions × average customer value.

    For this model, a “lead” means an acquired customer. If you enter a lead conversion rate instead, enter average revenue per lead as the value to keep the estimate consistent. AI-attributed visits are not measured here.
