    10 / Understand the search landscape

    SEO gets you ranked.
    GEO gets you recommended.

    Two ambitions, shared foundations. SEO and GEO work towards visibility in search results and generated answers. Rankings, citations and recommendations are never guaranteed.

    SEOSearch Engine Optimization
    Improve how search engines discover, understand and present your pages in organic results.
    Focus: crawlable pages, useful content and clear site structure.
    GEOGenerative Engine Optimization
    Work to improve how your content is represented or cited in generated answers. It overlaps with SEO and does not guarantee a recommendation.
    Focus: clear information, supporting evidence and observed citations.
    AEOAnswer Engine Optimization
    A working term for making content useful as a direct answer to a specific question. It overlaps with SEO and GEO rather than defining a separate channel.
    Focus: concise answers, explicit definitions and useful context.
    AI SearchAI-assisted search experiences
    Search experiences that use AI to interpret questions and assemble answers, sometimes with links to supporting sources. This is a discovery channel, not a standalone optimization technique.
    Focus: understand the questions asked and review the answers shown.

    For Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, existing SEO foundations remain relevant. Google specifies no extra technical requirements or special AI markup for eligibility. Inclusion is not guaranteed.
